Our Businesses

One group, four disciplines on the ground

RV2 Energy's metering contracts are the core of the business — but the same field workforce and operational backbone runs three other divisions across power, civils and new connections.

Div. 01

RV2 Energy — Metering

Our founding business: domestic and commercial meter exchange, service and repair, delivered across three major national contracts — Sureserve, SCM and SMS. This is where the majority of our 45-strong field team sits, covering smart meter installs, dual-fuel exchanges, and reactive repair work across Yorkshire and the wider region.

Metering is the discipline everything else at RV2 grew out of, and it's still where our compliance and quality systems are most mature — Gas Safe, MOCOPA and EUSR accreditation run through every job.

Div. 02

RV2 Power

RV2 Power handles electrical installation, testing and maintenance work — supporting network operators and commercial clients with the same field-first, compliance-led approach we apply to metering. Think fuse board and cut-out work, low-voltage installation, and electrical fault-finding on live sites.

It's a natural extension for engineers already working across dual-fuel metering who want to widen their ticket into full electrical scope.

Div. 03

Civils

Our Civils teams handle the groundworks side of utility work: excavation, reinstatement, and surface restoration for connections and infrastructure projects. Every metering or connections job that needs a hole dug, a duct laid, or a road surface put back properly runs through this team.

It's unglamorous, essential work — and having it in-house means our metering and connections teams aren't waiting on a third-party contractor to open or close a job.

Div. 04

ICP — Independent Connection Provider

Our ICP works cover new electricity network connections — designing and installing the infrastructure that connects new housing developments, commercial sites and industrial units to the grid, as an alternative to going direct through the regional Distribution Network Operator.

This is where our Power and Civils teams come together most directly: cabling, jointing and civils groundworks delivered as a single, accountable connections service for developers and commercial clients.
