Upskill

Qualified for the next rung, not just the job in front of you

Every engineer on an RV2 contract has a route mapped out — from first ticket to multi-skilled, from installer to supervisor. We fund and schedule the training; you bring the graft.

How it works

Three stages, one workforce

STAGE — ONBOARD

Get ticketed

We sponsor Gas Safe registration, MOCOPA and EUSR accreditation for new starters, alongside site-specific induction for your contract (Sureserve, SCM or SMS).

STAGE — MULTI-SKILL

Widen your ticket

Cross-train from single-fuel metering into dual-fuel, service and repair, or fabric-first work — so you're never limited to one job type on your patch.

STAGE — LEAD

Step into supervision

Structured pathway into Field Supervisor and Team Leader roles, covering audit, coaching and safety management, for engineers ready to run a patch of their own.

Accreditations we fund

Tickets we get you

GAS

Gas Safe Registration

Sponsored ACS assessment and re-certification for meter, service and repair work.

ELEC

MOCOPA

Meter Operation Code of Practice Agreement accreditation for electric metering work.

SAFETY

EUSR

Energy & Utility Skills Register cards covering safety passports across gas, power and water sites.

Why we invest

Because turnover costs more than training

Field workforce businesses live or die on retention. Every accreditation we fund keeps an engineer on our books longer, widens the work we can put in front of them, and means fewer gaps to cover on a Monday morning.

It also means the person turning up at a customer's door is properly qualified for the job — which is good for compliance, good for safety, and good for the reputation of every contract we run.

Who it's for

Every engineer on our books

Whether you're PAYE or CIS subcontract, on Sureserve, SCM or SMS — if you're doing the work, you're in scope for the next ticket. Speak to your Field Supervisor or check current openings to get started.
